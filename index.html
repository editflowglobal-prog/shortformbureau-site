01 · THE PROBLEM

Great episodes. No second life.

You publish long, valuable conversations. Most people will never press play on 60 minutes, and short-form is where they'll meet you first.

Cutting clips that land takes real time: finding the moment that stands on its own, opening on the hook instead of the setup, captioning it cleanly. Auto-clipping tools find loud moments. They don't find the ones that make your show look smart.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

Two episodes in. Twelve clips out.

DAY 0

Send two episodes

YouTube links or raw files, plus your logo, fonts and colors.

DAY 2

Approve the moment list

12 timestamps, each with a proposed hook. Keep or swap any of them before we edit.

DAYS 3–8

We edit

Hook-first opening, tight pacing, reframing to 9:16, clean audio, burned-in captions.

DAYS 8–10

Review and deliver

One consolidated round of changes, then final files in a shared folder, ready to post.

06 · FAQ

Questions.

Do you guarantee views?

No. Reach depends on the platform, your account history and timing. We guarantee the work: 12 well-edited clips, on time.

Who owns the clips?

You do. They're cut from your content and delivered to you with full rights to use them anywhere.

What do you need from us?

Two episode links (YouTube or raw files) and your brand basics. Raw multi-camera files are welcome but not required.

Do you post the clips for us?

In the pilot, we deliver ready-to-post files and you post them on your own channels. Nothing is posted anywhere without your approval.

Can we try one clip first?

Yes, as a paid sample: one finished clip for $75, delivered in 3 business days and credited toward the pilot if you continue within 14 days. We don't do unpaid spec work.

Which platforms?

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and LinkedIn, from one 9:16 master per clip.
